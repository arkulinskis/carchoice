When you find a car for sale, paste its link here instead of bookmarking it. Add the price, year and mileage and CarChoice shows which one is priced well.

Find your five for sale

How the price check works: for each model, listings in the same currency are put on the same footing, assuming a car loses about 12% of its value a year and 3% per 10,000 km, then compared with each other. It's a guide to spot the well-priced one among those you found, not a valuation.
