From five to one

  1. Find them for sale. Each card opens searches on the marketplaces of your region.
  2. Save the listings you like. Paste the link with its price, year and mileage.
  3. Let the numbers talk. Listings of the same model are put on the same footing (age and mileage), so the well-priced one stands out.
  4. Check before you pay. A history report, an independent inspection, and the price guides on Where to buy.
