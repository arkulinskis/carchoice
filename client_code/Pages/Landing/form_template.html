Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Exploring needs no account. A free account keeps your five, your saved searches and the listings you find, on any device.

Does CarChoice sell cars?

No. It points you to the marketplaces where people sell them. No listing is paid to appear, and CarChoice takes no cut.

How accurate are the specs?

They are approximate figures for a typical trim, gathered from what manufacturers publish. Trims, model years and markets differ, so check the maker's spec sheet for the exact car you buy.

Which countries does it work in?

The catalogue covers cars sold in North America, Europe, the UK, Australia and Asia. Ready-made searches cover marketplaces in the US, Canada, the UK, Europe, the Baltics and Australia.

What does the price check do?

For each model, it puts the listings you saved on the same footing (about 12% of value per year of age and 3% per 10,000 km) and compares them with each other. It helps you spot the well-priced one among those you found; it isn't a valuation.

A car or a figure is missing. Can I tell you?

Please do, with Submit feedback at the bottom of the page. Published data you can point to (a brochure, a spec sheet, a dataset) is the most useful.
