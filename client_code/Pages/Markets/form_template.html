1. Know the going rate. Compare with a price guide and with the other listings you saved for the same model.
  2. Get the history. A vehicle history report (Carfax or AutoCheck in the US, Carfax Canada, an HPI check in the UK) shows accidents, write-offs and mileage records.
  3. Check the VIN. It should match the papers, the windshield plate and the report.
  4. Look for recalls. Open recalls are free to fix at a dealer.
  5. Pay for an inspection. An independent mechanic finds what photos hide.
  6. Meet safely, pay safely. See the car and the papers before sending money. Never pay a deposit to a seller you haven't met.

Prices on marketplaces are asking prices: there is often room to negotiate, more with private sellers.
